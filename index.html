Performed in sequence

The Order of the Rite

Face East. Move slowly and deliberately; each name is vibrated — intoned so it resonates through the body. The whole rite is a descent of light, then a circle of flame, then a return.

  1. I

    The Qabalistic Cross

    With the right hand, draw light down and across the body, marking the cross of the Tree of Life upon yourself.

    • AtahTouch the forehead — “Thou art.”
    • MalkuthDraw down to the breast — “the Kingdom.”
    • Ve-GeburahTouch the right shoulder — “and the Power.”
    • Ve-GedulahTouch the left shoulder — “and the Glory.”
    • Le-Olam, AmenClasp the hands at the heart — “to the ages, Amen.”
  2. II

    The Four Pentagrams

    Turning clockwise, trace a banishing earth pentagram in each quarter — beginning at the lower-left point, up to the crown, and around. Stab the centre and vibrate the divine Name, carrying a line of light from one quarter to the next until the circle is closed.

  3. III

    The Evocation of the Archangels

    Return to face East. Extend the arms to form a cross, and call the four Archangels to their stations.

    The Six-Rayed Star

    Seal the working: “For about me flames the Pentagram, and within me shines the Six-rayed Star.” Then repeat the Qabalistic Cross to close, and the circle stands complete.

On the practice

Why Practitioners Return to It Daily

The LBRP is the first ritual most students of the Western esoteric tradition learn, and the one they keep. Beneath its ceremony lies a compact discipline of attention — a way of clearing, centering, and orienting oneself before deeper work.

  • ✦Clearing the space. It sweeps stray, distracting energies from the room and the aura, leaving a clean field for meditation or magical work.
  • ✦A ring of protection. The sealed circle establishes a felt boundary against unwanted influence, and with it a settled sense of safety.
  • ✦Trained concentration. Precise gestures married to vibrated Names build focus and mental discipline the way scales build a musician.
  • ✦Elemental balance. Calling Air, Fire, Water, and Earth to their quarters harmonises those forces within the practitioner.
  • ✦A grounding rhythm. Repeated daily, it becomes a reliable frame that steadies the nervous system and deepens meditative states.
  • ✦Subtle attunement. Over time the rite sensitises the practitioner to finer currents of energy and attention.
  • ✦Psychological integration. Working with divine Names and Archangels engages and reconciles different parts of the psyche.
  • ✦Foundation for more. It is the grammar upon which the more advanced rituals and operations are built.

Woven into a daily practice, the LBRP gives students of magick and meditation a stable, protected, and balanced ground from which to grow.
