Welcome to the interactive 2D animated guide for performing the Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram (LBRP). This visualization tool will help you learn and practice this fundamental magical ritual.

Ritual Steps

1. The Qabalistic Cross

The animation will guide you through the following movements:

  • •Touch your forehead: "Atah" (Thou art)
  • •Touch your chest: "Malkuth" (Kingdom)
  • •Touch your right shoulder: "Ve-Geburah" (and the Power)
  • •Touch your left shoulder: "Ve-Gedulah" (and the Glory)
  • •Clasp your hands at your chest: "Le-Olam, Amen" (Forever, Amen)

2. The Pentagrams

Watch as glowing pentagrams are drawn in each cardinal direction:

  • •East: Yellow pentagram
  • •South: Red pentagram
  • •West: Blue pentagram
  • •North: Green pentagram

3. Calling the Archangels

Observe as each Archangel is invoked:

  • •East: Raphael
  • •South: Michael
  • •West: Gabriel
  • •North: Uriel

4. Completion

The animation concludes by showing all four pentagrams glowing simultaneously, forming a protective circle.

The Value of LBRP for Students of Magick and Meditation

The Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram (LBRP) is a cornerstone practice in Western esoteric traditions, offering numerous benefits to students of magick and meditation:

  • • Energy Cleansing: LBRP helps clear negative energies from your space and aura, creating a clean slate for magical work or meditation.
  • • Psychic Protection: It establishes a protective barrier against unwanted influences, enhancing your sense of safety during spiritual practices.
  • • Concentration Enhancement: The ritual's precise movements and visualizations improve focus and mental discipline.
  • • Elemental Balancing: By invoking the four elements, LBRP helps balance these forces within the practitioner.
  • • Meditative Framework: The ritual's structure provides a consistent, grounding practice that can deepen one's meditative experiences.
  • • Energetic Attunement: Regular practice attunes the practitioner to subtle energies, enhancing overall magical sensitivity.
  • • Psychological Integration: Working with divine names and archangels can facilitate integration of various aspects of the psyche.
  • • Preparation for Advanced Work: LBRP lays a foundation for more complex rituals and magical operations.

By incorporating LBRP into their daily practice, students of magick and meditation can create a stable, protected, and energetically balanced foundation for their spiritual growth and magical endeavors.
